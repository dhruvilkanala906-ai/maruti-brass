About MARUTI BRASS

Empowering Global Engineering with Superior Quality Brass Components

Manufacturing Excellence

End-to-End Production Capabilities

From raw brass rod extrusion to precision multi-axis CNC machining, surface finishing, and automated optical inspection.

CNC & VMC Turning Centers

High-speed Swiss-type sliding head automatics and multi-axis CNC lathes capable of maintaining tight tolerances up to ±0.005mm for complex automotive and medical parts.

  • Outer Diameter: 1.0mm to 150mm
  • Live tooling for milling & cross-drilling

Hot Forging & Pressing

Heavy friction screw presses and automatic temperature-controlled induction heaters for dense, pore-free brass forgings used in high-pressure gas valves and plumbing.

  • Press Capacity: Up to 350 Tons
  • Complex hollow and solid forgings

Electroplating & Finishing

Fully automated plating lines providing uniform coatings for corrosion protection, electrical conductivity, and premium architectural aesthetics.

  • Finishes: Nickel, Tin, Chrome, Silver, Satin
  • Salt-spray test verified up to 96+ hrs
